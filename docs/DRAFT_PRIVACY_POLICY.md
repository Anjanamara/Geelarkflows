# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 22, 2026  
**Operator:** GeeLark Flows (`geelarkflows.com`), United States  
**Contact:** support@geelarkflows.com

---

### 1. Overview
**GeeLark Flows** ("we", "us", "our") operates `geelarkflows.com`. We limit the information we collect and process to information used to operate the storefront, process payments, deliver orders, provide support, handle custom requests, maintain security, and meet applicable operational or legal requirements.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral analytics.

---

### 2. Information We Process

#### 2.1 Orders & Delivery
When you place an order, we process:
- **Email Address**: Used to communicate order confirmations, delivery files, setup coordination details, and support responses.
- **Order Details**: Order reference, selected workflows, quantities, subtotal, delivery method, and fulfillment status.

#### 2.2 Cryptocurrency Payments
Payments are handled through our payment processor, **NOWPayments**:
- We process payment metadata such as the selected cryptocurrency network, generated deposit address, payment amount, invoice status, and blockchain transaction hash.
- We do not collect or store private wallet keys, credit card numbers, or bank account details.

#### 2.3 Custom Automation Inquiries
When you submit a request for custom development:
- We collect your name, email address, and project requirements to evaluate feasibility and provide quotes.
- For abuse prevention and rate limiting, we derive and store a pseudonymous technical identifier from the connecting IP address. The application does not persist the raw IP address with the request. **Please note: Pseudonymous data is not anonymous data.**

#### 2.4 Customer Communications
When you email `support@geelarkflows.com`, we process your email address, message content, and any files or screenshots you provide to help resolve your inquiry.

#### 2.5 Browser Local Storage
We use browser local storage solely for basic site functionality:
- To retain your cart items across page refreshes.
- To keep your active cryptocurrency payment screen and QR code accessible if you refresh during checkout.
- We do not use marketing or advertising cookies.

#### 2.6 Infrastructure & Administrative Records
- Our hosting and edge infrastructure (provided by Cloudflare) handles standard network connection information necessary for security, routing, and delivery.
- Administrative session and audit records may include security identifiers, IP addresses, user-agent information, and actions performed in the administrative system for operator authentication and audit logging.

---

### 3. How We Use Information

Information is used strictly for operational purposes:
- Delivering purchased automations and setup instructions.
- Verifying cryptocurrency payment settlement.
- Coordinating GeeLark Account Setup services.
- Responding to support inquiries and troubleshooting technical issues.
- Reviewing custom automation requests.
- Protecting the website against abuse and spam.
- Maintaining operational, accounting, payment, and legal record-keeping.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on technical infrastructure providers to operate the storefront:
- **Cloudflare, Inc.**: Hosting, edge network, database, and asset storage.
- **NOWPayments**: Cryptocurrency invoice generation and payment status monitoring.
- **Resend, Inc.**: Transactional email delivery and inbound support email processing.

---

### 5. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain operational and payment records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, satisfy legitimate business requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where information is no longer reasonably needed, it may be deleted or de-identified.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13.
