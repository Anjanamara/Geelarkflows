# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** August 22, 2026  
**Operator:** GeeLark Flows (`geelarkflows.com`), United States  
**Contact:** support@geelarkflows.com

---

### 1. General Policy for Digital Deliverables

All products sold on **GeeLark Flows** (`geelarkflows.com`) are digital software packages, automation workflow scripts, and technical setup services.

Because these are digital deliverables, **purchases are generally final and non-refundable once the digital workflow package has been successfully delivered, accessed, or sent to your supplied email address**, except where otherwise required by applicable law.

---

### 2. Situations Eligible for Review

We will review refund or replacement requests in the following circumstances:

1. **Duplicate Payments**: You accidentally submitted duplicate payments for the same order reference or invoice.
2. **Non-Delivery**: A confirmed paid order that GeeLark Flows is ultimately unable to deliver after reasonable support and fulfillment efforts.
3. **Wrong Product Delivered**: The digital package sent does not match the product specified in your order confirmation.
4. **Material Defect at Delivery**: The workflow has a material technical defect present at delivery that prevents execution on the specified platform as configured, and the issue cannot reasonably be corrected.

---

### 3. Support & Correction-First Approach

Because automation workflows operate in external browser and app environments:
- If you experience an unexpected configuration or execution issue after delivery, please contact **support@geelarkflows.com** with your Order ID and relevant error details.
- Where a technical issue can reasonably be diagnosed and resolved, GeeLark Flows may first provide **technical support, workflow corrections, or an updated replacement package** prior to issuing a refund.

---

### 4. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are not granted for:
- Changing your mind or no longer needing the automation after delivery.
- Third-party platform, website, or mobile application changes occurring after successful delivery.
- Account restrictions, action limits, shadowbans, suspensions, or similar actions imposed by third-party platforms.
- Failure to achieve particular business, audience, revenue, or follower results.
- Purchasing the wrong workflow by mistake once the package has already been provided.

---

### 5. GeeLark Account Setup Services

For orders that include **GeeLark Account Setup** ($50 setup fee for workflow subtotals under $300; waived for subtotals $300 or greater):
- **Work in Progress**: Once setup work has materially begun in your designated GeeLark environment, setup service fees are generally non-refundable unless we cannot perform the agreed service or applicable law requires otherwise.
- **Customer Cooperation**: Setup requires customer cooperation to provide configuration details. If required details are not supplied, the order remains **pending customer cooperation** until received.

---

### 6. Cryptocurrency Payments & Refunds

#### 6.1 Blockchain Irreversibility
Purchases are settled in Tether USD (USDT) on supported blockchain networks (TRC-20, ERC-20, BEP-20, or Solana). Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible once confirmed.

#### 6.2 Network & Token Selection
Customers must send the exact required amount to the payment address generated for that order on the selected network. Incorrect-network or unsupported-token transfers may be unrecoverable, and recovery cannot be guaranteed.

#### 6.3 Approved Crypto Refunds
If a cryptocurrency refund is approved, GeeLark Flows will provide the required refund instructions through support. Approved refund amounts may be affected by applicable:
- Blockchain network gas fees
- Payment-provider transaction fees
- Exchange-rate changes
- Reasonable recovery costs
